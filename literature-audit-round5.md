# Literature audit, round 5 (2026-10-17)

Scope: what is already known about (L)/(R) on Cayley-Dickson basis units, alternative twisted group algebras / quasialgebras, and the pentagon exchangeability and necessity results. Citations are for attribution, not as evidence. No priority claim is made. Most sources were read only through fetch extracts or abstracts, so every statement is "per extract" and should be checked against the full paper before it is cited in print.

## Known
- Kinyon, Phillips, Vojtechovsky, *C-loops: extensions and constructions*, arXiv:math/0412390, section 7: the loop L_n of signed standard basis elements of the real Cayley-Dickson algebra satisfies the alternative and flexible laws for every n (Prop. 7.1: L_n is a flexible C-loop). They cite their Lemma 6.2(vii) and prove it only in part. Our (L) is the left alternative law x(xz) = (xx)z and (R) is the right alternative law on this loop. For dimension >= 32 the loop is neither Moufang nor commutative; its nucleus is {+-1}.
- *Loops with involution and the Cayley-Dickson doubling process*, arXiv:2501.00123: doubling of loops with involution; Aut(Q_n) = GL_3(F_2) x {+-1}^(n-3) for n > 3; Prop. 9.5: for loops with involution, left- or right-alternative implies alternative; Cor. 10.7: for a normal involution, the double is Moufang iff the base loop is associative.

## Related, not the same
- Albuquerque, Elduque, Perez-Izquierdo, *Alternative quasialgebras*, Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 63 (2001) 257-268: Prop. 3.9(i) phi(a,b,c) = 1 iff the subgroup generated by a,b,c has order <= 4, for alternative division quasialgebras. Closest analogue of our rank-2 statement, but a different setting (division quasialgebras over a field versus arbitrary normalized cochains on F_2^n, and the sign loop of the Cayley-Dickson algebras is alternative while the algebra is not, from n = 4).
- Albuquerque, Majid, math/9802116: octonions as a cocycle twist of the group algebra of Z_2^3 (abstract only).

## Not found
The pentagon-edge exchangeability statement, the Gram-matrix argument, and the necessity theorem. The searches were few and the phrasing niche; this is weak evidence and supports no conclusion about novelty.

## Open
Read in full: KPV sections 6-7, arXiv:2501.00123 sections 9-10, AEP 2001 section 3. Look for the earliest statement of alternativity of the Cayley-Dickson unit loop (Culbert, Cawagas, Kivunge-Smith). Earlier unfinished items: Bales 1707.07318, Schafer, Imaeda.
